# A new look, and the game now speaks Russian

Two things landed together, and they turned out to be the same problem.

## Russian

Wordliz now plays in Russian — interface, board words, and definitions. It is
not a translation layer over an English game: Russian has its own word list and
its own dictionary of meanings, so the boards are dealt from Russian words and
every word you turn up explains itself in Russian.

Board codes carry their language with them. If someone sends you a Russian board
while you are playing in English, it opens in Russian and plays exactly as it did
for them — same words, same shuffle. Nothing is uploaded; the code still rebuilds
the board from a single number.

## A new look

The old design was a cool grey. The new one is a felt table: linen background,
bone tiles with real weight to them, and colours that mean something — the green
of a banked word, the amber of right-letters-wrong-order, the red of a word that
is real but not one of yours. The same three colours in both light and dark
themes, so the board reads the same either way.

The wordmark is laid out in tiles now, like the board. Tap it.

## Where the two met

Fitting the two together turned up something worth mentioning: the typeface the
redesign was built on had no Cyrillic at all. Every Russian letter on every tile
was quietly falling back to whatever sans-serif your system happened to have —
the whole design applied to the English version only. The game runs on Rubik now,
which covers both alphabets, so the two versions are finally the same game.

The fonts ship with the game rather than loading from a CDN, which also means the
board looks right the first time and keeps looking right offline.

## Smaller things

- Dragging a row with a mouse no longer smears a text selection across the board,
  and the board shows a grab cursor, so it is obvious the lines can be pulled.
- Both dictionaries had a clean-out. Some words were being explained by the wrong
  meaning entirely — "gore" was defined as the vice president, and in Russian
  «волк» came out as a given name rather than the animal. Around 1300 definitions
  are now the right ones.
- The points line in the menu counts properly in Russian, and the version stamp
  on the stats screen says it is a version instead of just showing a date.

Free, in the browser, no accounts, works on phones. Same as ever.
