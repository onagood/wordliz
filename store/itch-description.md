# Wordliz — itch.io page copy

## Short description / tagline
Every letter is already on the board. Slide whole rows and columns until the hidden words line up.

## Description

**Wordliz** is a sliding word puzzle. The board holds several hidden words,
scrambled together — every tile belongs to one of them, nothing is spare.
Drag rows and columns — whole lines move, wrapping around the edges — until
each word clicks into place. Putting one word right pulls the others apart,
and that is the entire game.

### Reading the board

- 🟩 **Green** — a hidden word. It banks itself.
- 🟨 **Amber** — right letters, wrong order. Reorder the row, don't break it up.
- 🟥 **Red** — a real word, but not one of the hidden ones. Worth a point for ruling it out.

Only hidden words can be banked. Everything else is information.

Every word you turn up, banked or ruled out, shows what it means — the little
dictionary ships with the game, so nothing is looked up online.

### Two languages

Play in **English** or **Russian**. Each language has its own word list and its
own dictionary of meanings; the interface follows. Board codes carry their
language with them, so a board someone shares opens in the language it was
dealt in, whichever one you happen to be playing.

### Four ways to play

- **Daily** — one 5×5 board for everyone, once a day. Compare move counts fairly, keep your streak.
- **Free** — no clock, three board sizes (4×4, 5×5, 6×6). Your score is your move count.
- **Rush** — the clock is ticking and every banked word buys less time than the last. How deep can you go?
- **Custom** — your board, your rules: 2–6 words of 4–6 letters, how many letters show from the start, an optional clock, hints on or off.

### Every board is a code

A board fits in a short code like `PS9WXB` — copy it from the pause or end
screen, send it to a friend, and typed into the menu it rebuilds your exact
shuffle: same words, same scramble, in any copy of the game. Custom codes
start with `C` and carry your rules along with the board.

Finish a game and your share becomes a **challenge code**: whoever enters it
plays your exact board with your score to beat — fewest moves, or most words
in Rush — gets a verdict at the end, and can fire a challenge straight back.
Duels with anyone, through any messenger, no accounts.

No accounts, no downloads, works on phones. Free.
