## Short description / tagline
Every letter is already on the board. Slide whole rows and columns until the hidden words line up.

## Description

**Wordliz** is a sliding word puzzle. The board holds several hidden words,
scrambled together — every tile belongs to one of them, nothing is spare.
Drag rows and columns (whole lines move, wrapping around the edges) until
each word clicks into place.

- **Green** row — a hidden word. It banks itself.
- **Amber** row — right letters, wrong order.
- **Red** row — a real English word, but not one of the hidden ones. Worth a point for ruling it out.

**Three ways to play**
- **Daily** — one 5×5 board for everyone, once a day. Compare move counts fairly, keep your streak.
- **Free** — no clock, three board sizes (4×4, 5×5, 6×6). Your score is your move count.
- **Rush** — the clock is ticking and every banked word buys less time than the last. How deep can you go?

Every board comes from a short code like `PS9WXB` — share it and a friend
lands on your exact shuffle. No accounts, no downloads, works on phones.
